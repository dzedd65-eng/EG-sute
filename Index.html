Life at Emerald Gardens

What a day actually looks like

Not a brochure schedule — this is the real rhythm residents settle into, built around comfort, routine, and small joys.

7:30 AM

Morning & medication

Gentle wake-up support, personal care assistance, and medication management handled by trained staff — at each resident's own pace.

8:30 AM

Breakfast together

Home-style meals in the dining room, with modified diets and personal preferences accommodated by our kitchen staff.

Evening wind-down

Levels of Care

Care that adjusts as needs do

We assess every resident individually and build a service plan around their actual needs — not a one-size package.

Standard Care

For residents who are largely independent and want community, support, and peace of mind.

  • Private or shared room
  • Three home-style meals daily
  • Housekeeping & laundry
  • Medication reminders
  • 24/7 staff on-site

Enhanced Care

For residents who need more hands-on help with daily living while staying engaged and social.

  • Everything in Standard Care
  • Assistance with bathing, dressing, mobility
  • Medication administration
  • Incontinence care support
  • Care plan reviewed regularly

Memory Care

A secure, structured environment for residents living with Alzheimer's or related dementia.

  • Secured, purpose-designed wing
  • Specially trained memory care staff
  • Structured daily routine & redirection
  • Family support & education
  • Higher staff-to-resident ratio
